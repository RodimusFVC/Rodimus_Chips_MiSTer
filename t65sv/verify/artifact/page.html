6502-family CPU core · SystemVerilog · Cyclone V / Quartus 17

t65sv

One synthesizable core. Mode "00" is cycle-for-cycle and pin-for-pin T65 Ver 315 (NMOS with the undocumented opcodes); parameters add the 6510, 6507, 2A03, three 65C02 flavours and the 65C816.
